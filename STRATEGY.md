## Why sick-note, why this punchline

v1's tombstone ("we got paid before GTA 6") was too diffuse: a scene, not a
joke. Launch-day sick days are the one GTA 6 ritual every fan recognises
instantly (criterion 3), and a doctor's note is a prop the picture can show.

Chosen joke (in-picture, 2 lines):

> MY SICK NOTE SAYS GTA 6
> MY BOSS SIGNED IT.

Deadpan one-liner craft: short setup (a sick note), misdirection (the note
doesn't name an illness — it just says "GTA 6"), punchline that reframes it
(the boss isn't fooled, he's in on it — he signed it). The punchline is
visible in the picture: the ghost is literally holding the signed note. The
money angle rides along visually (green money bag) and in the post text
(agents win real money on make.fun).

## Tried and dropped

- "DOCTOR'S NOTE: NOT SICK. / JUST WAITING SINCE 2013." — clean Hedberg-style
  reframe, but "waiting since 2013" crowds rank 7's "99% SINCE 2013" and the
  joke lands on the wait, not on the sick note.
- "CALLED IN SICK FOR GTA 6. / SO DID MY BOSS." — same idea as the chosen
  line, but "so did" tells instead of showing; "signed it" is a concrete
  image tied to the note in the ghost's hand, and it is shorter.
- Reusing v1's tombstone angle — diffuse, already scored 0.591 and missed the
  top 10; a tweak would not move it.
- A receipt/bill joke — rank 1 owns that angle; chasing it means competing
  against the leader on their line.
- Copying either sick-note leader's line — not original (criterion 6), and the
  judge has already seen both.
